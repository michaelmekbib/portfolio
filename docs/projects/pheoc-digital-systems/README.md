# PHEOC Digital Systems

I led digitization and data-management capacity building for public health emergency operations centers across Africa.

**Focus:** Emergency preparedness, information systems, training, and outbreak monitoring  
**Tools:** Public health information systems, Power BI, project management

## The challenge

Emergency operations teams need connected systems, reliable reporting, and the capacity to use information during rapidly changing responses.

## My contribution

I led PHEOC digitization and data-management training across **23+ African Union member states**, supporting the use of information systems and dashboards for emergency operations.

This work contributed to Africa CDC’s broader PHEOC strengthening initiative supporting **31 member states**, with activities spanning digital equipment, information systems, training, and project coordination.
