# Modernizing CVX Effective Dates

I developed an editorial framework and AI-assisted evidence workflow to document when vaccine products represented by CVX codes were available for administration.

**Status:** Ongoing; validation and expert review in progress  
**Tools:** Python, CVX/NDC terminology, regulatory records, historical research, AI-assisted synthesis

## The challenge

Historical vaccine availability information is distributed across regulatory records, marketing data, and historical documentation. Bringing these sources together helps answer a core question: was the vaccine product available when a historical administration was recorded?

## My contribution

I led the capstone design and developed the evidence hierarchy, decision tree, editorial rules, and effective-dating workflow. The approach connects five stages:

1. Inventory CVX codes and review NDC mappings.
2. Collect regulatory and historical evidence.
3. Apply standardized editorial and adjudication rules.
4. Derive proposed dates through a structured pipeline and expert review.
5. Publish machine-readable outputs and maintain them as products change.

The framework accommodates legacy products, non-US vaccines, unspecified formulations, and codes without traceable NDC information. For US-use timelines, end dates reflect the last documented year of US use, supported by historical evidence.
