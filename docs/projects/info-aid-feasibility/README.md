# Info Aid Feasibility Assessment

I assessed SharePoint and Microsoft Power Platform as a foundation for modernizing continuing education accreditation workflows.

**Status:** Feasibility assessment completed  
**Platforms:** SharePoint, Dataverse, Power Apps, Power Automate, Microsoft 365

## The challenge

The accreditation process relied on emailed forms, manually routed approvals, spreadsheet tracking, and repeated entry across documents. The team needed to determine whether the recommended platform could support a more connected and maintainable service.

## My contribution

I applied a Government Accountability Office-informed assessment approach, mapped workflow requirements to platform capabilities, and identified the decisions needed before development.

## Findings

| Dimension | Rating | Key finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical | High | Strong fit with the proposed data and workflow model. |
| Governance | Conditional | Requires decisions on tenant policy, identity governance, and external access. |
| Operational | Conditional | Requires ownership, development capacity, environments, and support arrangements. |
| Scalability | High | Supports expansion with clear roles and appropriate configuration. |
| User adoption | High | Requires training and support for collaboration and review. |

My conclusion was that the platform is feasible, with implementation dependent on governance, access, licensing, and operational readiness.

## Modernization approach

I outlined a future workflow connecting digital intake, parallel approvals, automated notifications, reporting, document reuse, version control, and audit records. A shared activity record provides the foundation for consistent information across the accreditation lifecycle.

## Recommendations

Before development, confirm the tenant and connector environment, external-access model, licensing, internal technical capacity, and workflow crosswalk. Assign service ownership and establish support arrangements for continuing operation.
